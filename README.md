01 - Data Source

The underlying dataset is:

Australian Bureau of Statistics — Education and Work, Australia, May 2025

02 — The Business Question

For an education-sector organisation, understanding the education market means looking beyond enrolment numbers.

The more strategic questions are:

-Who is participating in education?
-What qualifications does the population already hold?
-Which fields of study are gaining or losing share?
-What does the apprenticeship pipeline look like?
-How engaged are young Australians in study and/or employment?
-How is the qualification base changing over time?
-Where do meaningful differences exist across Australian jurisdictions?

This dashboard brings those questions into one executive-facing analytical view.

The fictional decision-makers are an education-sector CEO and CFO, with the dashboard framed as strategic market intelligence rather than an official statistical publication.

The dashboard follows a deliberate progression:

03 - Key Analytical Signals

The dashboard surfaces several notable patterns from the underlying data.

1.Qualification depth is increasing

Non-school qualification holders increased from approximately 10.49 million in 2016 to 12.87 million in 2025.

2.Higher-level attainment is becoming increasingly female-skewed

Female bachelor-degree-or-above attainment remains above male attainment throughout the 2016–2025 series, with the gap increasing from 4.3 percentage points to 7.3 percentage points.

3.Technology is gaining study share

Information technology's share of current study increased from 3.2% to 5.6% between 2015 and 2025, making it the strongest-growing field highlighted by the dashboard.
